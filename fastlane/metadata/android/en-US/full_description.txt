A fork of the Android port of 3D Pinball for Windows – Space Cadet, tuned for the Anbernic RG Rotate and other small screens.

- Ball-following camera modelled on Pokémon Pinball: Ruby & Sapphire (GBA), with a score and mission bar
- Pokémon Pinball (GBA) gamepad layout: D-pad Left / A flippers, B launch, L / R nudge, Start pause, Select new game
- Local high score table; no online leaderboard and no analytics
